# Code of Conduct

OpenDataFusion is committed to a welcoming, professional and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or
employer.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Give actionable review feedback and accept it with the same intent.
- Respect project boundaries, maintainer decisions and private security
  reports.
- Keep discussions in the agreed project channels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information, credentials or customer data.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior, spam or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests,
chat and events where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in
[CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
warn, remove content, restrict participation or ban an account, depending on
severity and repetition. A maintainer with a conflict of interest must step
aside from the decision.

This document is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
